# Design System — Sonora Digital Platform

## Visual Theme
Clean, premium, Apple-inspired dark interface. Minimal chrome, content-forward.
Alternating sections with deep black canvases and subtle dark surfaces.

## Color Palette
- Background: `#000000` (primary), `#0a0a0a` (surface)
- Surface: `#1a1a1e` (card), `#252529` (elevated)
- Accent Blue: `#0071e3` (actions, links, focus)
- Text Primary: `#f5f5f7`
- Text Secondary: `#6e6e73`
- Border: `#2a2a2e`
- Success: `#22c55e`, Warning: `#f59e0b`, Error: `#ef4444`

## Typography
- Font: Inter, -apple-system, sans-serif
- Hero: 48px/1.1 semibold
- Heading: 24px/1.2 semibold
- Body: 15px/1.5
- Small: 12px/1.4

## Components
- Buttons: pill shape (9999px radius), blue fill for primary, ghost for secondary
- Cards: subtle dark surface, 12px radius, 1px border
- Inputs: dark surface, blue focus ring, 12px radius
