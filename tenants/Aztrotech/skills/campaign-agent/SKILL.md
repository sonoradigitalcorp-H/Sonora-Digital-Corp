# Campaign Orchestration Agent — Aztrotech

## Overview
AI agent that orchestrates entire marketing campaigns for clients. Manages outreach, follow-ups, and lead nurturing across multiple channels.

## Capabilities
1. **Lead Segmentation** - Segment leads by type (cold/warm/hot), industry, location
2. **Campaign Creation** - Create personalized campaigns per segment
3. **Multi-channel Outreach** - WhatsApp, Email, Telegram, Instagram
4. **Follow-up Automation** - Scheduled follow-ups based on lead response
5. **Market Study** - Analyze market by geographic area
6. **Performance Tracking** - Track conversion rates, response rates

## Campaign Types

### Cold Leads (Score 0-30)
- **Objective**: Educate and nurture
- **Channel**: Email + WhatsApp broadcast
- **Frequency**: 1x per week
- **Content**: Educational, case studies, benefits

### Warm Leads (Score 31-70)
- **Objective**: Engage and convert
- **Channel**: WhatsApp direct + Telegram
- **Frequency**: 2x per week
- **Content**: Demos, testimonials, special offers

### Hot Leads (Score 71-100)
- **Objective**: Close the deal
- **Channel**: WhatsApp direct + Phone call
- **Frequency**: Daily until response
- **Content**: Personalized proposals, urgency

## Geographic Segmentation
- **Hermosillo**: Primary market
- **Culiacán**: Expansion
- **Mazatlán**: Tourism businesses
- **Los Cabos**: High-end clients
- **National**: Remote clients

## Campaign Workflow
1. Analyze lead database
2. Segment by type + location + industry
3. Create personalized message templates
4. Schedule outreach via WhatsApp/Email
5. Track responses and update lead scores
6. Escalate hot leads to César
7. Generate weekly performance report
